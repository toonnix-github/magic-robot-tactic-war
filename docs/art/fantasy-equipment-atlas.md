# Fantasy paper-doll atlas

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-07. Source copied into `assets/fantasy/equipment-atlas.png`, preserving its original alpha channel. No external runtime image dependency. Region coordinates and shared pose registration are owned by `src/ui/fantasy_paper_doll.gd`; compositing occurs at runtime. The output was visually inspected and actual regions measured because the generator did not precisely follow the requested uniform cell registration.

This is static prototype art. Gear variants can share layers. The spear and wand currently reuse the staff silhouette; finer equipment-specific artwork is future work.

## Registration correction

Weapons now rotate around measured source grip points registered to the base body's palm, with uniform scaling to preserve their silhouette. The sword angles downward/outward, the staff and bow lean outward, and the shield centers on the other hand. A foreground crop of the existing wrist/fingers establishes grip occlusion without modifying the raster source.

Armor uses measured collar and belt landmarks registered to the shared body's neck and waist, with independent garment-width fitting. The robe skirt extends below its registered belt without stretching the upper torso. Constants are owned by the paper-doll view and do not alter gameplay or build data. `fantasy_alignment_acceptance.gd` checks registration against independent source measurements. `tools/capture_fantasy_alignment.gd` produces a six-case equipment contact sheet including the uncovered body reference.
